The challenge

Casey Margenau's team has closed more than $4B in career sales. The site that represented them did not carry that weight.

It had to speak to buyers and sellers of high-value homes at the same time, surface the numbers that build trust without turning into a brochure, and do all of it without looking like every other agent template in the market. The bar was the homes themselves: quiet, confident, expensive in the right way.

What we built

A single page that does the work of five. It opens on a full-bleed photograph under a deep navy scrim, with a light-weight Inter headline doing the talking and three career numbers sitting under it. A hard-edged red accent marks every call to action, and nothing else.

  • Two paths, one glance. Sellers get a navy panel, buyers a white one, side by side — each with its own four-point promise and its own next step.
  • A featured property with a price badge and an address bar, ready to swap as listings change.
  • Credentials in a grid. Career volume, homes sold, CRP certification and the in-house staging team, laid out like a plaque rather than a stat bar.
  • How we work, in four numbered steps on navy — listing preparation, luxury marketing, pricing and negotiation, contract to closing.
  • Testimonials, FAQs and the latest insight to answer the questions a first visit raises, and a closing band with the office address and one phone number.

The result

A homepage that reads as luxury from the first frame and keeps reading that way on a phone. Three layout options were delivered so the team could choose the hero treatment; the structure is built to grow into listings, community pages and the blog without a redesign.
